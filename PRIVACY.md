# Decision Drift Guard: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-04. Publisher: E:LAB STUDIO (Kyoungchan Yang), Seoul, Republic of Korea. Contact: hello@elab-studio.com

Decision Drift Guard is an Atlassian Forge app. It runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform.

- **What it reads:** the summary, description and labels of the Jira work item being checked, and Confluence pages labelled `decision` on the same site.
- **What it writes:** a Jira comment and the label `decision-drift` when a new work item breaks a recorded decision rule; a Confluence footer comment and the Jira label `decision-reopen-requested` when a user asks to reopen a decision. It never edits a decision page.
- **What it stores:** nothing. The app has no database, no Forge storage, and no external servers. No data leaves the Atlassian platform (no egress).
- **AI:** the in-panel check is rule-based. The optional Rovo agent runs on Atlassian Rovo under your site's Rovo settings; the app does not send data to any other AI service.
- **Personal data:** the app does not copy or store account IDs or other personal data.
- **Removal:** uninstalling the app stops all processing. Comments and labels it added remain in your site like any user-created content and can be deleted there.
